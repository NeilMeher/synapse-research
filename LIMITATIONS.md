# Limitations

- A single exchangeable mixture yields identical original signals during a shared regime. Other joint distributions can have the same pairwise correlation and different voting outcomes.
- Binary truth, homogeneous known signal reliability and synthetic uncertainty constrain interpretation. No human mechanism, empirical treatment effect or market prediction was measured.
- The default hub's terminal weight exceeds 0.5 and its signal dominates the decision. Complete and ring means are invariant. The fixed bounded-confidence radius prevents any initial cross-belief interaction.
- Verification replaces three slots with equally reliable independent signals. Known source structure is an oracle advantage in the matched Bayes comparison. This provides no estimate of the cost or efficacy of real fact-checking.
- Monte Carlo intervals quantify sampling error under the generator. Independent reference code was authored in the same session. Human review and external peer review are pending.
- Random, small-world, scale-free and community network families, dynamic trust, sequential cascades, AI agent interventions, empirical data and cross-lab experiments remain proposed.

ECHO 2.0 matches full exchangeable pairwise correlation matrices. Its finite moment bounds need not be attainable under a latent independent-report mixture. Heterogeneous marginal reliability, clustered dependence, source uncertainty and empirical evidence are outside this protocol.

The integrated study controls marginal source accuracy, pairwise correlation, allocated capital and expected order size between like-sized mechanisms. Order volume can still differ in individual episodes. Quote impact is one market parameter. Independent human or empirical validation is open. The result does not characterize financial system instability.

## Phase IV

NOESIS's archived likelihood assigns Gaussian noise to a noiseless first observation, and its coefficient prior differs from the generating scales. The archive's in-domain prediction grid overlaps the candidate query sites. SCIENTIA has known variances and prespecified dependence. One BH decision changes with an exact numerical tail. EPISTEME is restricted to a four-family dictionary, a particular misspecified family, and known Gaussian observation noise. The posterior is conditional on that dictionary. Passing both checks is an operational result at sampled inputs. The project has no independent human peer review and uses neither human participants nor evaluated LLM participants in these studies.

## Phase V

AXIOM's v1 certainty comes from a finite mathematical scope. Empirical framing, social preferences and human moral reasoning remain open. GAME OF CIVILIZATIONS and POLIS prescribe payment, topology, output rates, visibility and decision rules. Their observed effects require those assumptions. The pooled records are selectively generated by attempted transactions. Agent welfare and Gini are computed from two equally weighted resource quantities. The simulations do not contain emerging governance rules. Human and independent external scientific review remain pending.

ARTHA assumes fixed input coefficients and prices, nationally unconstrained capacity, and no change to direct imports. It reports value added separately from gross output and estimates no employment or causal policy impact.

ENERGIA has no project-specific tariff, quotes, funding commitment, taxes, refinancing or site feasibility. Negative payment coverage and scheduled equity requirements are modeled indicators. No calibrated probability of default is provided.

NEXUS uses stipulated producer-value prices and domestic procurement shares with no measured margins, labor responses, regional estimates, joint cooling feasibility or causal effects. Additional price, commissioning and supplier evidence is needed before an empirical or site-specific study.

CHRONOS uses sparse historical benchmarks and a recent annual national series. Different publisher totals, ambiguous state boundaries, and missing compatible local exposure and outcomes limit causal use. The narrow educational counterfactual and exact synthetic contrasts are separate from historical evidence. See the [central register](research/programme-quality-register.md).

## ECHO public-archive studies

This retrospective analysis uses seven checkpoint configurations, two English subjects and a fixed answer extractor. Model and original benchmark snapshot hashes were unavailable. The pinned canonical benchmark contents match archived item documents. Context limits and Transformers versions differed and the generation cap can couple format failure with measured accuracy. Trained data overlap and contamination are unknown. Error correlations do not identify model mechanisms. Multiple secondary results are descriptive. The higher-order pair shares two members, so it can neither isolate a higher-order effect nor support a model-population claim. There are no comparable expressed-confidence measurements. Human peer review and external replication remain open. [Audit](research/echo-public-audit.md)

## Second-generation ECHO and MORPHEUS

The English–Hindi archive changes prompt language, translations, stop strings and the response filter together. The publisher's expert-rating table omits Hindi. Question structures and numeric anchors were screened blind. The discovered omitted statements and unmatched pairs remain documented. Independent bilingual item review, population claims, and fresh committee-selection confirmation remain open. The moment bounds condition on fixed calibration moments and have separate sampling uncertainty. MORPHEUS trusts independent delayed audit feedback and generates a stylized binary state. Its improvement during a source fault coexists with worse recovery and slower clean adaptation.
