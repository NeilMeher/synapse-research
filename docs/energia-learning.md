# ENERGIA learning guide

In the [workbench](../energia.html#workbench), vary workload utilization while holding spot billing utilization fixed. Higher active power raises kWh and water. Then vary billing utilization while holding engineering workload fixed. The resulting difference follows from the scenario's separate workload and contract assumptions.

Change preset to all equity. The project NPV stays equal to the baseline, while scheduled equity NPV changes and DSCR becomes inapplicable. Compare the baseline and end-year replacement. Read the dated reserve draw, debt balance and equity funding in the ledger. The end-year option still consumes cash.

For a pencil-and-paper interpretation see [energy and power](../academy.html#power-energy) and [infrastructure project finance](../academy.html#project-finance). The [registered evidence](../energia.html#evidence) can be reproduced independently. Treat each cell as a conditional comparison under the frozen assumptions.
