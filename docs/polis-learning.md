# POLIS learning guide

## An expected payoff can include one party or both

An offer costs the payer 2 utility units of surplus and yields 3 units if delivery succeeds. With belief p, expected payer gain per unit is 3p−2. The supplier receives a unit useful for meeting need with value 3. When it delivers its own surplus, it gives up value 2. Its expected gain is 3−2p. Joint expected gain is therefore 1+p. In the POLIS replay those objectives feed the same AXIOM best response function.

## Why a policy label does not identify a mechanism

**Exercise.** At p=1/2, what would the pooled material policy and the pooled joint policy do? Could an unaware fixed-prior payer take the same action as the joint policy?

**Solution.** The pooled payer anticipates −0.5 per unit and declines. The joint policy anticipates +1.5 and accepts. The unaware payer uses p=3/4, giving +0.25, and accepts. The latter two take the same action for distinct computational reasons.

## Check distribution

In the replay select a policy, move to the last day and inspect the difference for each agent against the matched material world. Sum individual changes and compare directions. Across all archived worlds, the paired protocol summarizes fulfilled need, counts of improvements and losses, Gini of consumption and shock-day outcomes.
