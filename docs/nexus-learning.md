# NEXUS learning guide

Open [the matched workbench](https://neilmeher.github.io/synapse-research/nexus.html#bridge) and move the domestic-share control without changing the efficiency assumption. Find the change in the electrical, cooling and network lines in the procurement table. The accelerator line remains entirely a direct import. Use the supplier selector to see the most important first-round buying relationships.

Reset the share to the original value and choose the lower-intensity design. The same capital assumes identical hardware and purchasing shares, so the supplier requirements stay fixed. Facility energy, water, electricity cost and project cash flow can change. Explain why a higher modeled NPV still does not imply financial viability.

Review the [input–output module](https://neilmeher.github.io/synapse-research/academy.html#input-output), [power and energy module](https://neilmeher.github.io/synapse-research/academy.html#power-energy), and [optimization lesson](https://neilmeher.github.io/synapse-research/academy.html#optimization). Use the [registered comparison](https://neilmeher.github.io/synapse-research/nexus.html#evidence) to see the four matched cases and the [concordance](../data/processed/nexus-capital-map.json) to audit the spending assumptions.
