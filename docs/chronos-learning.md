# CHRONOS learning guide

Open the [national workbench](https://neilmeher.github.io/synapse-research/chronos.html#series). Compare originating freight and tonne-km in 2023–24 and 2024–25. Explain how higher freight volume can coincide with less transport work. Change to the annual-growth view to see where CHRONOS requires an adjacent full year.

Browse March state stocks for 2013, 2014 and 2015. Explain why summing or comparing the published Andhra Pradesh and Telangana series around the boundary is unsafe. Inspect a selected national year to locate the data on the primary Railway Board page. For the assignment's treatment timing, compare the trial and dedication dates and consult the [causal readiness register](../research/chronos-causal-readiness.md).

In the synthetic exercise, compare post-only, before–after and DiD estimates. Change the treated group's untreated trend and add a spillover into the control in separate cases. The truth remains four. Explain why this synthetic ground truth is available and why the national railway data cannot supply it. The separate descriptive share exercise changes an assumed growth fraction and keeps route totals fixed.
