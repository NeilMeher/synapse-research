Release status follows mathematical, experimental, statistical, software, website, documentation and recorded review gates. Later milestones are proposed.

Phase II begins with ECHO 2.0 matched-moment dependence, a rational reference and distributional bounds. The next vertical slices are MIRAGE matched confidence, REFLEX deterministic market accounting, and CONVERGENCE. Each requires its own frozen design and computed evidence.

The first scoped MIRAGE, REFLEX and CONVERGENCE milestones now have deterministic artifacts. Expand heterogeneity, source provenance, communication sensitivity and market horizon only under separate frozen designs.

Phase IV delivered NOESIS and SCIENTIA, with EPISTEME as their cross-laboratory validation experiment. The atlas, registry and five Academy modules are application-level integration. Human scientific review remains pending. A new NOESIS acquisition benchmark could compare actual expected information gain with the archived heuristic under a distinct protocol and held-out grid.

The next proposed new laboratory is AXIOM. Start with payoff-preserving equivalence or relabeling tests on small two-player games, explicit deterministic and stochastic decision policies, and independently calculable reference controls. Define utility, framing, equilibrium and cross-game consistency before running a pilot. Design any empirical or language-model arm as a separate study.

The first AXIOM work is scoped in [the payoff-preserving design](research/axiom-design.md). Implement canonical payoff and belief permutations and validate response mapping on the exact 20 task and 80 presentation fixtures before freezing the pilot.

The Phase V AXIOM, barter and POLIS benchmarks are complete within their registered synthetic scope. A narrow follow-up should vary payment enforcement and surplus valuation before extrapolating from the current policy equivalence. ARTHA, ENERGIA and CHRONOS remain separate proposed milestones. ARTHA's first future task is to identify official versioned Indian supply-use or input-output tables and their units and licensing before implementing a model.

Phase VI ARTHA national modelling is implemented. State and district modelling requires consistent regional purchase and trade data. Next validate ENERGIA, then use its explicit procurement ledger to test a narrow NEXUS integration.

ENERGIA's initial engineering and finance study is validated on hypothetical inputs. A later site-specific extension needs approved tariffs, power capacity, water permits, independently verified quotations, regulated lending review and workload data.

After validated Phase VI publication, CHRONOS is the next research milestone. Pin the source editions for Indian rail operations, electrification, and state route length. Reconcile observations and coverage before testing whether local outcomes can support a design with credible comparison groups. [Initial design](research/chronos-source-checkpoint.md)
