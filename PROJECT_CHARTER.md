# SYNAPSE · Computational Intelligence and Complex Systems

SYNAPSE is an independent open-source research programme. It asks how individual cognition becomes collective intelligence and how collective intelligence shapes economic and social systems. It begins with an auditable computational experiment about a bounded model.

The programme links four divisions and twelve proposed laboratories. ECHO launches first so later laboratories can reuse experimental design, scenario replay, deterministic seeds and artifact provenance. The other labs remain proposals until they earn their status through checked models and experiments.

Label quantitative claims as established theory, verified observation, published reproduction, original computation, synthetic output, exploratory evidence, assumption, hypothesis, or unresolved. Establish novelty against prior work before claiming it. Keep results fallible, expose analytical checks and report contradictions. Any human-subject study needs a separate authorized protocol and appropriate ethics review. Public operation is static and the initial authorized cash budget is zero.

The 26 November 2026 SYNAPSE V2.0 charter supplied by Neil Meher governs long-term scope. This file records the project commitments that apply to the first implementation.
