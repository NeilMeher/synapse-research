# POLIS 2.0 pilot protocol

Run 32 matched worlds from master seed 427965817, keeping the original POLIS 24-day labor stress condition with eight ring agents, equal-unit resource intake and constrained storage. Match each world across the six combinations of two payment rules and three surplus values, with four decision rules per combination. The simulation consumes preassigned choice and potential delivery draws for each pair and day, even if no trade is attempted, so alternate policy paths never shift the world schedule.

Only exchange valuation and settlement change. Keep the original production objective, needs, physical inventory and funding rules. The payer has a unit cost s under surplus valuation s in {1,2,3}; a delivered unit fills an unmet need valued at 3. The supplier receives a needed unit and sends a surplus unit. Under prepayment, the payer transfers even on a failed delivery and gains 3p−s in expectation. The supplier gains 3−ps. Under payment on delivery, both units transfer atomically after success, both inventories stay unchanged after failure and both agents have expected gain p(3−s). There is no fee, enforcement process or reservation. The two parties at a meeting are disjoint from other meetings that day. The supplier permits any funded offer because its expected gain is nonnegative in this range, including ties.

The material rule compares the buyer's expected gain to zero. Joint gain sums both agents' anticipated gains. The cautious rule uses pooled material gain and refuses any supplier with an observed failure. The fixed-prior rule uses material gain at p=3/4. Other beliefs come from (3+previous successes)/(4+previous attempts), with separate evolving records per arm. Both exact arithmetic and a matched uniform choice resolve each indifference. No chooser accesses the assigned future outcomes.

Record world-level need, sorted Gini of total individual intake, worst-served intake, attempts, rejections, completions and two-way plus one-way transfer units. Within cell, compute individual differences from the matched material arm, number and fraction of agents worse off, and largest and conditional mean loss. Summarize conditional loss only in worlds with at least one agent losing intake. Store the first world's arm results, exact accounts and observed joint/fixed-prior physical-equality counts. The planned confirmatory contrast is the interaction of payment and joint-minus-material fulfillment at original s=2. In the pilot all intervals are descriptive. Use the fixed budget to assess precision, cost and invariants before freezing a separate confirmatory seed.
