# ENERGIA source verification and limitations

| Source | Verified use | Exclusions in the registered scenario |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NVIDIA L40S | Manufacturer lists 350 W maximum power consumption at device boundary. | The assumed 65 W idle and 320 W active under load are illustrative. Server overhead, network/storage loads, availability, rack design and redundancy need hardware and commissioning evidence. |
| U.S. Department of Energy, July 2024 design guide and FEMP cooling guidance | PUE compares site and equipment energy. The site water indicator uses IT energy as denominator. Dry cooling and evaporative cooling can trade electrical for water use. | Scenario PUE and WUE values are stipulated. The comparison makes no cooling vendor performance claim. A single PUE applied to peak kW is a design approximation. |
| MSEDCL tariff archive, Maharashtra | The official archive includes the MYT order in Case 217 of 2024 and its Case 75 review materials. Applicable billing needs current distribution area, voltage, approved category, contract demand, actual peak and applicable duties, time-of-day charges and surcharges. | No actual tariff, open-access charge, power-factor penalty or reliability estimate is used. The model's energy, demand and fixed prices are editable scenario dollars. |
| Maharashtra Industrial Development Corporation | Its official land and allotment interfaces support an initial inventory of potential industrial locations. | Plot and development terms, sanctioned load, access, fiber, equipment siting and grid offers need separate verification. No parcels are ranked. |
| Central Ground Water Board, Maharashtra 2024 | State and taluka-scale assessment provides documented regional context. | An assessment cannot verify a plot's legal water supply. The modeled site water volume excludes upstream water, withdrawal permissions and discharge accounting. |
| RBI Project Finance Directions and lender-specific directions | The 19 June 2025 RBI announcement set an October 2025 effective date. Check current lender-specific credit facilities and stress rules before using an actual transaction. | The model is an all-equity and scheduled senior-debt comparison on hypothetical terms. It establishes no institutional eligibility or securities pathway. Sector, borrower and investor classifications, debt availability, security and covenants require professional review. |

[NVIDIA specifications](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/l40s/) · [DOE design guide](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2024-07/best-practice-guide-data-center-design.pdf) · [DOE cooling and water](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/femp/cooling-water-efficiency-opportunities-federal-data-centers) · [MSEDCL approved tariff archive](https://www.mahadiscom.in/consumer/en/tariff-details/) · [MIDC](https://www.midcindia.org/) · [CGWB Maharashtra report](https://www.cgwb.gov.in/cgwbpnm/public/uploads/documents/17482445781522785056file.pdf) · [RBI source](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=60678)
