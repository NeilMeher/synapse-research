# ECHO 2.0 mathematics

Let $K=\sum_i C_i$ count correct reports in an exchangeable population of size $n$. Conditional on $K=k$, all binary vectors with $k$ successes have equal probability. A particular vector has mass $p_k/\binom nk$. ECHO keeps an equiprobable binary truth and symmetric correctness.

The shared-source law uses a common Bernoulli($q$) report with probability $\rho$ and independent reports otherwise. The beta-binomial law draws $P\sim\mathrm{Beta}(q(1/\rho-1),(1-q)(1/\rho-1))$ and then draws conditionally independent Bernoulli($P$) reports. Total covariance gives $\mathrm{Cov}(C_i,C_j)=\mathrm{Var}(P)=\rho q(1-q)$. The exact sampler integrates out the beta draw using the predictive probability $(q(1-\rho)+\rho k)/(1-\rho+\rho t)$ after $t$ observations with $k$ successes. The first draw uses $q$. Dynamic programming propagates count mass with the same recurrence. The endpoints are independent and one common report, respectively. The independently written Python reference instead uses exact rational rising factorials.

## Moment constraints and sharp bounds

Set $\mu=nq$ and $s=n(n-1)[q^2+\rho q(1-q)]+\mu$. The feasible distributions have nonnegative $p_k$, $\sum p_k=1$, $\sum kp_k=\mu$, and $\sum k^2p_k=s$. For integer support $0,\ldots,n$, $s$ lies between the lower piecewise-linear envelope of $k^2$ at $\mu$ and the upper line $n\mu$. This is necessary and sufficient. Majority credit is 0 below half, 1 above half and 1/2 for a fair tie. Minimize and maximize its expectation. A bounded feasible LP has optimal extreme points supported on at most three counts, since four positive weights permit a nonzero perturbation conserving all three moments. The weight at $i$ on a triple $i,j,k$ is $(s-(j+k)\mu+jk)/[(i-j)(i-k)]$. Zero weights include degenerate pairs and singletons. Published rational reference witnesses are checked exactly. These are bounds for finite exchangeability, which includes a wider class than infinitely extendible mixtures. At $\rho=0$, exchangeability and zero correlation can still permit dependence.

## Forecasts and influence

For truth one versus zero the likelihood reverses each block's count. Combinatorial factors cancel in the odds. Correctly specified forecasts know the true law, and misspecified ones use a declared alternative. Fresh reports are modelled as independent and may have a different known reliability. If both likelihoods vanish the forecast is undefined and its event mass is reported. If the assumed model assigns zero probability to the outcome, its log loss is infinite. JSON reports explicit statuses, with no clipping of forecasts. Calibration conditions on the model's confidence in its selected answer, and bins carry their probability mass.

For the symmetric hub write $a_t$ for the hub share in the final unweighted mean after $t$ rounds. Then $a_0=1/n$, $a_{t+1}=0.5+(h-0.5)a_t$ and stationary share is $a_* = 0.5/(1.5-h)$. The finite value is $a_t=a_*+(1/n-a_*)(h-0.5)^t$. If $a_t>0.5$ at symmetric nondegenerate initial beliefs, the hub determines the decision even when every leaf disagrees. Equality can produce a tie. Complete and ring preserve the mean under fixed doubly stochastic matrices. Their intermediate disagreement can differ. For a given count $k$, the hub correct probability is $k/n$, which lets the exact calculation average the two possible hub states.

Verification has an explicit synthetic utility $U=\Pr(\mathrm{correct})-mc$, where $m$ slots are replaced by independent signals, a wrong answer costs one unit, and each replacement costs $c$. All compared policies use the same total number of reports. This does not model empirical verification costs.
