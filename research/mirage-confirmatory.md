# MIRAGE confirmatory protocol

Freeze this protocol before executing the new run. An exploratory 350-case paired pilot with 500 training observations took 9 ms. The paired standard deviations for style minus trained Brier were about .219 and .215 across the two regimes. At n=6,000 the projected Bonferroni interval half-width is below .007. New seeds 511201 for 3,000 normal training cases and 511202 for testing differ from both pilot seeds. The study is a controlled synthetic test of calibration and discrimination.

Each task draws a fair binary truth, one of three cue-quality tiers uniformly, and independent primary, hint and descriptive-style draws. The first-order choice always equals the primary cue. Use the same primary choice and truth for every policy and regime. Keep the hint draw fixed across conditions and reverse its correctness rate in the deceptive regime. This is distribution shift in a declared source, not a claim about real misinformation detection.

Evaluate a constant training rate, independent style confidence (.65 or .95), a nominal Bayesian forecast with declared hint reliability, a Laplace-smoothed estimate from the six training cells, and an oracle using true hint probabilities. The target is the probability that the already selected answer was correct. The oracle knows the model parameters but receives no current outcome. Only the calibration policies differ.

The two prespecified contrasts are style minus learned Brier in each regime. Use paired task differences and two-sided 97.5% normal intervals, yielding familywise 95% Bonferroni coverage in the asymptotic design. Expect lower Brier for trained calibration under normal cues. Inspect the shifted contrast regardless of sign. Descriptive secondary results are matched accuracy, NLL, 10 equal-width reliability bins and ECE, AUROC for detecting error from low confidence, and risk at 10%, 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% coverage. Break confidence ties uniformly in the coverage expectation. Log loss can be infinite for hard wrong forecasts, which are explicitly labelled. Report all policies and the first 12 paired tasks. Stop at the set sample size or fail on input validation, discrepancies with the finite-cue check or 30-second budget. No external models, subject data or cost.
