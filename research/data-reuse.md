# Data and source reuse register

## ADB national accounts

The release contains information from ADB's India national input-output table series, © Asian Development Bank. ADB Data Library specifies CC BY 3.0 IGO for its published data unless indicated otherwise. KIDB serves the precise newer workbook under publisher-specific terms. The Phase VI source manifest pins the workbook and records the link from the earlier licensed dataset to the newer KIDB series. The existence of the general license does not settle any source-specific third-party right or restriction.

The production bundle and archive retain only the established, attributed numerical extraction and reproducible derivatives. The unmodified workbook, authorship metadata, publisher art and branding are excluded. The verification uses the exact pinned original from ADB. Keep current math, provenance and attribution together, and seek workbook-specific confirmation before broadening the distribution. [ADB terms](https://data.adb.org/terms-use-data) · [ADB KIDB source](https://kidb.adb.org/globalization/current) · [Pinned ARTHA source](../sources/artha-adb-india.json)

## Railway publications

Railway Board permits accurate attributed reproduction under its copyright policy with third-party exclusions. Its broader portal terms read differently. CHRONOS includes factual cells, with attribution, from the Board’s own summary and a limited numerical state subset explicitly credited to the RBI and the Ministry of Railways. Original PDFs, HTML and complete RBI tables stay with publishers. The Indian Ministry of Finance Economic Survey policy requires permission to reproduce site content. Only links and summary reconciliation commentary enter the release for those comparators. [Railway audit](chronos-source-audit.md) · [Ministry policy](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/website-policies.php)

## Other materials

The release does not include proprietary NVIDIA imagery, academic paper reproductions, government map artwork or externally sourced route geometries. ENERGIA cites source-backed facts and public definitions. Browser research code and project documentation are distributed under the repository's license. Markdown and attributed source entries retain individual scope. Browser automation and the PDF parser are verification dependencies outside the runtime bundle. [Repository license](../LICENSE)

## MMLU-ProX archive-derived evidence

The Hindi extension uses 15 targeted files from the pinned CC-BY-4.0 archived prediction dataset and the declared MIT benchmark publisher. The release gives credit, linked source, digests and a change description. Compact choices and source locators are reproduced from the openly distributed artifacts. Original question text, prompts and generated explanations stay outside the repository and the deployed site. [Publisher attribution and use limits](echo-hindi-alignment.md)

The prediction publisher is gililior and the benchmark publisher is li-lab. SYNAPSE filtered the cohort, independently extracted ordinal choices and published paired summaries. The original artifacts retain their respective licensing and attribution.
