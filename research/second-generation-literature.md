# Second-generation literature and contribution map

Use this map to distinguish research precedents, technical reproduction, bounded extensions and proposed evidence. The laboratory register links exact local studies and controls. These comparisons establish traceable starting points. Dedicated final literature review remains a manuscript gate.

| Laboratory | Verified study, year and venue | Method, evidence and main finding | Limitation and relationship to SYNAPSE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MIRAGE | Tian et al., 2023, EMNLP | Compares verbal forecasts and conditional token probabilities on TriviaQA, SciQ and TruthfulQA in instruction-tuned systems. Verbal forecasts often improved calibration. | These task formats and model capabilities differ from the controlled MIRAGE local runs. M1 is a measurement synthesis of fixed contrasts. Agreement-to-correctness calibration is a separate ECHO–MIRAGE methodological extension. [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.330/) |
| MORPHEUS | Dawid and Skene, 1979, Applied Statistics. Adams and MacKay, 2007, preprint. | The first estimates observer error from repeated ratings with a latent class model and EM. The second tracks a Bayesian posterior over time since change. | The supervised delayed audit and finite window used here require a different observation process. MO1 is a controlled method comparison. Change detection without independent verification remains open. [Observer reliability](https://doi.org/10.2307/2346806) · [Online change points](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.3742) |
| AXIOM | McKelvey and Palfrey, 1995, Games and Economic Behavior | Logit responses enter mutual fixed-point equilibrium conditions instead of best-response correspondence alone. | AXIOM fixes opponent probabilities and checks representation invariance. The next stochastic comparison needs equivalence stated at the distribution level. This is a technical reproduction fixture and potential method extension. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1006/game.1995.1023) |
| ECHO | Kim, Garg, Peng and Garg, 2025, ICML. Prékopa, 1990, Operations Research. | The empirical study measures shared wrong answers on several benchmarks. The mathematical study bounds Boolean event probabilities by linear optimization given partial likelihood information. | ECHO's empirical extension uses a different archived panel and grouped test. Higher-order bounds are an application and exact certificate package. No general claims of novelty for observing correlated errors or solving the moment problem follow. [ICML paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/kim25e.html) · [Moment bounds](https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.38.2.227) |
| GAME OF CIVILIZATIONS | Axelrod and Hamilton, 1981, Science | Examines conditional cooperation under repeated interactions and identifiable partners. Strategy success depends on interaction structure. | The conserved resource economy distinguishes settlement, reputation visibility and who bears losses. Repeated-game insights motivate the design without determining its outcomes. The next experiment is a synthetic institutional mechanism extension. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.7466396) |
| CHRONOS | Callaway and Sant’Anna, 2021, Journal of Econometrics | Identifies group-time treatment effects with staggered timing under parallel-trends and comparison assumptions. | The archived Indian aggregate series has no compatible segment panel. CHRONOS is a primary-source reconstruction and identification audit. A distinct causal study requires commensurate geography, period and comparison units. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2020.12.001) |
| NOESIS | Lindley, 1956, Annals of Mathematical Statistics | Defines expected experimental information through prior-to-posterior change, enabling design comparisons. | Corrected NOESIS should compare expected information under the true likelihood with disagreement and passive acquisition. Exact finite reproduction comes first. Misspecified acquisition and independent confirmation form the methodological extension. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177728069) |
| SCIENTIA | Benjamini and Hochberg, 1995, Journal of the Royal Statistical Society B | Gives a step-up rule controlling false discovery rate under the independent-testing setting studied. | Repeated adaptive selection changes the observation and testing process. The follow-up compares matched observation budgets and independent confirmation, and preserves the historic numerical tail discrepancy. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x) |
| REFLEX | Kyle, 1985, Econometrica | Sequential auctions aggregate noisy and informed demand through an endogenous price rule. | REFLEX prescribes impact and verifies cash and inventory exactly. Calibrated markets require new price formation and microstructure evidence. ECHO-to-REFLEX experiments are conditional mechanism extensions. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.2307/1913210) |
| ALPHA DECAY | McLean and Pontiff, 2016, Journal of Finance | Compares predictive returns out of sample and after publication for 97 characteristics. Reports decay across those comparisons. | Publication time is not a measured adopter headcount or deployed capital in the SYNAPSE simulator. A matched power-by-capital experiment is synthetic mechanism identification. Historical validation is separate. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12365) |
| ARTHA | United Nations Statistics Division, 2018, Handbook on Supply, Use and Input-Output Tables with Extensions and Applications | Sets out consistent source accounting and transformations between tables before modeling production requirements. | ARTHA uses an already symmetric ADB industry table. Its independent extraction and deterministic solver reproduce comparative statics with conditional prices and capacity. Identification of new industry substitution is proposed. [Handbook](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/nationalaccount/capdev/resources/details?resourceid=36&resourcetype=handbook) |
| ENERGIA | Masanet, Shehabi, Lei, Smith and Koomey, 2020, Science | Reconstructs global data center energy use from equipment, workload and efficiency trajectories. | Global energy accounting cannot supply individual site tariffs, cooling limits, water access or financing terms. ENERGIA and NEXUS provide a conditional scenario method and separate ledger and procurement transfers. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aba3758) |

For each proposed paper, the strongest alternative explanation is specified in the study protocol before drafting. For ECHO, differences in extraction, ability and response coverage can coexist with movement in dependence. For MORPHEUS, the static prior, external audit assumptions and source correlation can reverse the apparent merit of learning. For cross-laboratory transfers, an actual code-level mapping and a verified receiving model are required. Monograph synthesis will distinguish abstract roles from formal equivalence.
