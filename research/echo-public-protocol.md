# ECHO public-archive empirical protocol

The Limits of AI Consensus is a retrospective replication of error-dependence measurement and an extension to held-out committee selection. The two registered comparisons are ECHO public empirical 001 and 002. The publisher generated the archived model responses before this design. Archive metadata, structural cohort and canonical prompts were verified before this freeze. No aggregate scores or analysis outcomes informed cohort, vote or split selection.

The fixed panel contains the seven named configurations in the source manifest, drawn from five families. English economics and physics were selected for bounded availability, different kinds of questions, and file-by-file compatibility. Gemma and Llama each have two checkpoints so the family constraint can affect candidate selection. No model was executed by SYNAPSE. The unit of inference is a group of related canonical benchmark items.

The primary comparison uses full-plan paired accuracy of the fixed seven-member strict majority against the strongest individual selected on calibration. Invalid labels and voting abstentions count as incorrect on the full plan. Pairwise phi uses compatible matched denominators and also records valid-answer coverage, answer agreement and same wrong canonical choice conditional on both being wrong and valid. Failure count includes invalid responses. An error correlation may be undefined.

The 20%, 30% and 50% group split is frozen by subject and hash before extraction of analysis outcomes. Questionnaire sources and duplicate normalized question text are grouped by connected component. The development split is reserved for descriptive implementation checks and is excluded from selection or final estimates. Each committee-selection rule, the calibrated baseline and any higher-order match are locked in a separate calibration artifact before the held-out command runs. Every eligible three- and five-member subset is enumerated.

The categorical independence reference preserves varied option counts and distinguishes incorrect alternatives. The shown Monte Carlo rate is calculated from independent calibration fits. The re-pairing control samples one-to-one reassignments inside calibration strata and averages over the held-out distribution of subject and option count. It holds marginal answer patterns fixed within supported calibration strata. The Poisson-binomial reference addresses heterogeneous correctness directly. None of these calculations assumes the exchangeability or known generative moments used in synthetic ECHO 2.0. Conditional residual diagnostics use broad source groups as a proxy for item difficulty and support no causal explanation.

A single paired percentile interval covers the primary comparison. Secondary paired intervals and diagnostics are descriptive. There is no confidence-weighted condition. Coverage, invalid filter outputs, out-of-range labels, subject sensitivity, source-conditioned dependence and archive selection limits will be published even if the findings are negative.

The immutable execution parameters and deterministic tie rules are in [protocol.json](../empirical/echo-public/protocol.json). See the [source manifest](../empirical/echo-public/sources.json) and [alignment audit](../empirical/echo-public/alignment.json).
