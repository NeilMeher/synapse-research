The common model uses a latent state, an observation or report channel, an information set, a belief or forecast, a decision rule, and a loss or constraint. These are formal roles when a receiving engine implements a specific transfer. The source of state and meaning of loss remain domain-specific. CONVERGENCE implements ECHO reports into market order sizing, EPISTEME implements acquisition into validation, POLIS implements explicit valuation into audited exchange, and NEXUS maps ENERGIA procurement into ARTHA final-demand vectors. Each has passed its own scoped computation. Additional connections remain H until run.

The immediate publication candidates are a consolidated technical ECHO report on selective coverage and heterogeneous identification and a bounded MORPHEUS dynamic-trust study. The novel contribution must survive critical review. The programme research freeze, monograph and submission milestones remain later gates.
