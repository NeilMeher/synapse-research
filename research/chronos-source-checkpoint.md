# CHRONOS source discovery and design checkpoint

Historical checkpoint from Phase VI. See the [executed CHRONOS manuscript](../papers/chronos.md) and [source reconciliation](chronos-source-audit.md) for the current evidence.

## Initial direction

Start with an evidence reconstruction for Indian railway operations and electrification. The national series reports route length and freight with dated coverage footnotes. The state-level route series adds geographic granularity. Neither supplies the date each route was energized or a comparison group with a credible reason for a different exposure trajectory.

## Exact next step

Pin these editions and their attribution and reuse terms. Extract the overlapping yearly rail length and freight data with published units, fiscal calendar, missing values and series-break markers. Obtain and verify finer section-level commissioning records, then determine whether treatment timing and repeated local outcomes can be matched to a consistent unit before drafting any causal protocol. An observational event study would require defensible controls for earlier route investment, simultaneous logistics policies, pandemic effects, anticipatory behavior, spillovers and treatment selection. If usable timing or comparison groups cannot be documented, publish only descriptive reconstruction. No dataset has been harmonized and no causal or counterfactual study has been run.
