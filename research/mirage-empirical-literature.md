# MIRAGE empirical literature and comparison

This focused primary-source review informs the pilot. It supports a comparative extension of prior confidence and biased-cue work. No claim of priority is made.

| Primary source and full-text sections inspected | Models, cases and intervention | Outcomes and findings relevant here | Difference in MIRAGE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tian et al. (EMNLP 2023), pp. 5434–5438, methods, datasets and results | ChatGPT, GPT-4 and Claude on question answering including TriviaQA and SciQ. Compared verbal probabilities and conditional token scores under several elicitation strategies. | Verbalized probabilities often improved calibration in their tested settings. Strategy and task mattered. | Fixes one selected-answer confidence prompt and estimates a paired difference under misleading advice in an offline small quantized model. |
| Xiong et al. (ICLR 2024), §§3–5, Figs. 2–4 | GPT-4, GPT-3.5, GPT-3, Vicuna and Llama 2 across five categories, including arithmetic. Compares verbalized confidence, repeated sampling and aggregation. | Verbalized confidence often overstates correctness. Both calibration and identifying errors vary with strategy, domain and model. | Measures one pass per item and arm. Reports case-level missingness and a narrow complete-pair effect, with no retrained calibration or ensemble. |
| Turpin et al. (NeurIPS 2023), §§2–3, Figs. 2–4 | GPT-3.5 and Claude 1.0 across 13 BIG-Bench Hard tasks. Biasing cues include answer-position hints and social stereotypes. | Biasing inputs shifted answers, while generated explanations often omitted the bias. Their design focuses on explanation faithfulness. | A matched wrong recommendation is the perturbation. Outcome is a proper score on expressed probability, with no hidden reasoning or explanation collected. |

These are overlapping research questions rather than proof of novelty or evidence that the pilot will agree. Template dependence, grammar-constrained response support, one model and no correct-cue control limit transfer.
