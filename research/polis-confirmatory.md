# POLIS confirmatory protocol

The [pilot](polis-pilot.md) defined four policies and the primary outcome. It used 45 matched worlds per regime, took 786 ms and observed process RSS of 61,255,680 bytes. Stress-regime paired SD was 0.01870, projecting a 95% half-width of about 0.183 percentage points for 400 worlds. Confirmatory execution uses new master seed 543896211, 400 worlds at each of the same two scarcity levels, eight agents, the same 24-day schedule and unchanged policies. Freeze before inspecting any confirmatory outcomes.

The primary estimand is the mean paired difference in fulfilled need fraction between joint-gain pooled and material-gain pooled, in stress. Both arms allow access to the same category of delivery records. Changes to later realized records and stocks follow earlier decisions and belong to the treatment mechanism. Compare against zero using a two-sided paired normal 95% interval across independent worlds. Other outcomes and the moderate comparison are descriptive. The controls use the fixed-prior material rule and the failure-sensitive pooled rule. Pair each world across all treatments. Keep every gain, zero change, loss and null control in the archived reporting.

The joint-gain and fixed-prior mechanisms always accept funded offers by construction. Their physical outcomes should match exactly under identical potential draws. Their beliefs and displayed criteria can differ. The physical-accounting gates and information restrictions are inherited from GAME OF CIVILIZATIONS, with the AXIOM best response supplying choice probabilities. Record need, production, coinciding acceptance rules, completed and rejected trades, both Ginis, shock outcomes and individual gains and losses relative to the same pooled-material comparator.

In pilot checking, an exactly integral Python mean was compared as an integer to the binary64 online mean in the archive. The validated reference adapter applies the existing tolerance to summary means, while integer ledgers and replays are unchanged. Preserve the original pilot bytes. For confirmation run the deterministic archive check and `python -B scripts/check_polis_reference.py`. Stop at the fixed counts. Fail for reference, ledger or exact-control disagreement, hidden information access, or a runtime over the 30-second experimental limit.
