# CHRONOS causal identification readiness

The candidate estimand would be the post-commissioning effect of a segment-level freight intervention on recurring corridor freight throughput among served origin–destination markets. Establish whether commercial service began at the announced dedication, then define a prespecified exposure window. The current public evidence supports description and learning examples.

## Proposed causal diagram and future gate

Prior freight demand and industrial growth affect both infrastructure timing and later freight. Existing links and regional policy affect both. Corridor opening can change transport activity and reroute other facilities' traffic. The pandemic and contemporaneous logistics policy can affect throughput directly. These directed relationships are hypotheses to audit, and spillovers violate a simple independent-treatment model.

Before proceeding, require (1) a source-checked, commercially meaningful treatment log, (2) a matched outcome panel with comparable pre- and post-periods, (3) demonstrably eligible donor units, (4) a credible account of selection, bundled interventions and interference, and (5) a registered design with placebo/exclusion rules and inference suitable for the number of independent units. Public MoSPI aggregates can support descriptive geography, while restricted or panel access cannot be assumed. Current disposition is descriptive reconstruction and a separately labeled exact synthetic example. The hypothetical share scenario varies infrastructure accounting only.
