# EPISTEME confirmatory protocol

Independently of the pilot seed, use seed 2991051131 and 1,200 hidden-law tasks in each of the two regimes. The exploratory run used 100 paired tasks per regime and returned paired standard deviations of 0.545 for correct final claims and 0.426 for incorrect discovery proposals. At the new sample size, the projected adjusted half-widths are 3.52 and 2.76 percentage points. The two risk sets and outcomes were already defined in the pilot protocol. The design, Gaussian coefficient and observation distributions, grids, candidate families, selection windows and tests stay fixed. Both selection policies receive the same task, first observation, six discovery measurements in total, a fixed four-point validation block, and a different four-point replication block. The total observation budget is 14 per arm. Stop after the exact task count without interim looks.

In the closed-library regime, the first primary estimand is the paired difference in the probability of correctly naming an accepted family, disagreement minus random, after both disjoint checks. In the outside-library regime, the second primary estimand is the paired difference in the probability of a false discovery proposal, disagreement minus random, before those checks. The respective nulls are zero differences and the alternatives are two-sided. For both means use paired normal intervals with critical value 2.241402727604947 for Bonferroni family coverage of approximately 95%. Candidate probabilities apply within the dictionary. A posterior of at least 0.70 triggers a proposal. Keep each four-point residual block, measurement positions and threshold fixed. For the sharp hypothesis that the fitted prediction equals the hidden observation mean at those points, conditional on discovery the squared residual statistic divided by known variance is chi-square with four degrees of freedom. Otherwise it is noncentral and weak discrepancies can pass. A false proposal or acceptance is a named family unequal to the generator. Every named family is false in the outside-library cell.

Secondary outputs describe correct families before validation, false and correct claims after each gate, unresolved cases, disjoint noiseless-grid RMSE, mean time to first confident candidate with right-censoring coded as seven, and compute time and RSS. In the outside-library regime a lack of final claim is the appropriate operational outcome. The project uses synthetic laws with known variance. No empirical or human claims are planned.

Fail for a broken budget, grid overlap, unregistered design changes, failure to reproduce archives or replays independently, or a 30-second JavaScript execution budget overrun. The Python reference uses recursive Gaussian coefficient updates, while the JavaScript refits batch sufficient statistics.
