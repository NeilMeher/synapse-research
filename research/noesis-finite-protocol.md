# NOESIS finite discovery protocol

`noesis-corrected-discovery-002` · exact registered finite calculation · evidence A

Preserve the original Gaussian-law benchmark. Its active-disagreement comparisons against uniform and random acquisition were inconclusive. The original implementation starts with an observation with zero noise but uses the same noisy likelihood for it, draws fixed bounded coefficient ranges while fitting with a Gaussian parameter prior, and uses overlapping acquisition and test grids. Its active criterion is disagreement between predictive means. It does not enumerate prospective observations to calculate expected information gain. [Original audit](noesis-audit.md)

Here a family has prior 1/2, each of its two listed amplitudes has conditional prior 1/2, and both generating and inference laws use those probabilities. For each hypothesis-amplitude pair, the binary response at location \(x\) is independently Bernoulli with parameter \(1/2+a x^k\), using \(k=1\) or \(2\) according to family. The initial observation at 1/4 follows that same likelihood. Each procedure can acquire three distinct locations from five allowed points. The two held-out interpolation locations are −1/2 and 1/2, and the extrapolation locations are 5/4 and 3/2. The inference model can forecast at these design locations while actual evaluation outcomes and generator parameters are unavailable to acquisition. All domains have disjoint locations.

Uniform acquisition visits prespecified endpoints and an interior point. The other procedures choose greedily by disagreement, expected hypothesis information, expected joint information, expected interpolation Brier risk reduction, or expected focal decision value. Verify both possible observations with their correct predictive probabilities. A fixed candidate ordering resolves scores within \(10^{-12}\). The action concerns the distinct outcome at 1/2. False acceptance costs 4 and false rejection costs 3, with a rejection tie rule. The primary contrast is exact held-out decision loss for decision-value acquisition minus hypothesis-information acquisition in the specified model. Other metrics are diagnostic. All policies spend four observations and face the same acquisition domain.

Separate finite channel fixtures validate objective rankings before the main design. The study evaluates all 64 potential binary observation vectors per generating state. Each policy sees only the coordinates it acquired. Integrating the exact probabilities eliminates sampling noise and bounds the computation. The actual outcome at each evaluation point is integrated over its independent response law. No stochastic seed, tuning on evaluation outcomes or confidence interval is needed. Structural uncertainty about the finite law remains.

The out-of-library condition generates \(1/2+x^3/8\) under the same experimental design while leaving the inference model unchanged. Measure held-out Brier loss, excess Brier over the generator's oracle probability, decision loss and posterior mass above .75 on any available family. Family recovery and posterior mass of true family have no meaningful denominator when that family has prior mass zero.

The primary JavaScript scorer calculates entropy reductions from normalized prospective posteriors. The independent Python checker calculates mutual information from the joint law, multiplies all observed likelihoods from scratch, and compares every selected coordinate, posterior and held-out prediction. Bayesian design and information-versus-utility comparisons are established concepts. The contribution here is their controlled connection to a coherent discovery environment and a separate prediction domain.
