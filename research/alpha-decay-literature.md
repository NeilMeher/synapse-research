# ALPHA DECAY Literature Foundations

1. **Market impact and informed trading:** Kyle, A. S. (1985). *Continuous Auctions and Insider Trading*. Econometrica, 53(6), 1315–1335.
2. **Informationally efficient markets:** Grossman, S. J., & Stiglitz, J. E. (1980). *On the Impossibility of Informationally Efficient Markets*. American Economic Review, 70(3), 393–408.
3. **Strategy capacity and diminishing returns to scale:** Berk, J. B., & Green, R. C. (2004). *Mutual Fund Flows and Performance in Rational Markets*. Journal of Political Economy, 112(6), 1269–1295.
4. **Crowded trades:** Stein, J. C. (2009). *Presidential Address: Sophisticated Investors and Market Efficiency*. Journal of Finance, 64(4), 1517–1548.
5. **Execution and transaction costs:** Almgren, R., & Chriss, N. (2001). *Optimal Execution of Portfolio Transactions*. Journal of Risk, 3(2), 5–39.
