# ECHO archive alignment and scientific audit

## Provenance and access

The [gililior prediction archive](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gililior/mmlu-prox-eval-predictions/tree/7ed529332cc75dfa3700ee35cbfb802d54f779a3) is ungated and declares CC-BY-4.0. Its pinned card lists 25 checkpoints, 29 languages and 14 subjects. Hub tree metadata lists 10,877 files including two metadata files. The public viewer estimates roughly 9.12 million rows. This project checked the bounded slice directly. The [li-lab benchmark](https://huggingface.co/datasets/li-lab/MMLU-ProX/tree/8e6106a6c6ce1c5027e66cc338143cf997b2aa09) declares MIT, with upstream source rights applicable to any redistribution. The derived archive credits both publishers and describes its changes. It carries benchmark and source IDs, fingerprints, answer positions and derived statistics. Original question text, validation examples, full prompts and generated prose stay outside Git and Pages. The source manifest provides the pinned retrieval instructions and exact bytes and SHA-256 per file. No model weights or paid resources were used. ## Structural alignment

The full official English test Parquet has 11,759 rows and 14 subjects. The two selected subjects contained 2,143 rows, with each of the seven archived configurations present for each item. The initial three English economics files matched on benchmark document structure and run configuration. The expanded panel matched publisher IDs, original source IDs, category, question and option contents and order, full prompt, filter, and answer key across every retained observation. The independent raw replay checked all 14,994 compact predictions. Checked configuration and prediction file bytes also match each full Git blob identity. The canonical Parquet SHA-256 agrees with the publisher LFS object.

The one structural exclusion is English physics question 9754, with duplicated normalized answer-option content. All seven source rows are documented in the exclusion details. This gives 14,994 retained observations out of 15,001. There are no missing run rows, no cross-model rendered-prompt mismatches and no included publisher scoring disagreements. Eligible options vary from 3 to 10. All eligible votes are indexed on the matched ordered canonical options. A missed extraction and an out-of-range letter have separate missingness codes. Neither silently removes a question. The gold letter and independently extracted raw completion are checked for each record. ## Compatibility and interpretation

The task hashes and rendered prompts agree for the selected panel. The seven model repositories are explicit, with 5-shot validation prompts, bfloat16 vLLM, one sample, greedy settings and a 512-token cap. Olmo has a larger configured context. Qwen and the other six differ in Transformers version. The exact original runner dependency hashes, tokenizer snapshots, weight commits and benchmark revision are missing. Test document identity against the pinned official revision is an empirical alignment check. The publisher runs do not isolate model architecture, training, or the effect of a context budget.

Exact normalized question text and source identities were connected before partitioning. This catches repeated text and known source variants. Undetected paraphrases, benchmark contamination, public availability of the holdout, and overlapping pretraining data can persist. The subject grouping and two-subject sensitivity analyses give limited evidence about aggregate difficulty. Within-stratum dependence, model behavior changes under new prompts, and the effect of inference time remain open. Intervals condition on a single fixed panel and archive, allow between-group variation, and do not imply a probability over model populations.

The independent implementation validates the computation internally. Humans outside the study have yet to review it. The matched higher-order comparison shares two members and differs slightly in calibration and held-out moments. Confidence weighting remains unmeasured. The panel is sufficient for the registered 35-trio and 21-quintet searches. Model-level representativeness remains open.

## Next empirical checkpoint

Before and independently of any Hindi or Marathi ensemble results, verify source-question and translated-option identity for the seven existing configurations across English and each candidate language. Pin per-language files and inspect exact byte totals. Use only a scope covered by the existing resource authorization and validate that the same source group is kept together across languages. Freeze a paired language-sensitivity protocol after those checks and before evaluating language outcomes. Until then the multilingual extension remains proposed.
