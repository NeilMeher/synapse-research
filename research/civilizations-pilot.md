# GAME OF CIVILIZATIONS pilot

Run 60 independent trajectories at each of two scarcity levels with master seed 340220659. Each world uses eight agents on a fixed cycle. Alternating production technologies are [4,2] and [2,4] resource units per labor hour. Every agent needs [3,3] units each day and starts with zero to three units of each resource under the seeded draw. Four suppliers deliver 95% and four deliver 25% of the time after prepayment, with the types uniformly shuffled independently of inventory. The simulator scores reliability after selection. Decision policies observe only advertised surpluses and deficits and prior eligible delivery records. The reliability parameters and today's delivery draw remain hidden from them.

Each of 24 days proceeds through labor realization, production, disjoint adjacent encounters, consumption and storage. Common labor is two hours normally or one in a scarcity shock. The shock probabilities are 0.15 and 0.50 in the two levels. Production allocates whole hours to maximize the inventory score 2s + min(s,3), summed across resources, on the own post-production inventory. Ties select the first enumerated hours in increasing allocation to resource A. No cross-resource manufacturing or storage below the explicit cap is imposed.

Rotate the cycle by a random offset, pair consecutive sites and assign payer direction by a coin. Each person meets once per day. A proposed 1:1 trade requires complementary surplus above need and deficit on both ends, with quantity limited to two and the four advertised bounds. On attempted trade, the payer transfers its promised units. The second leg occurs when the supplier passes its delivery draw. A failed delivery leaves the first leg in the recipient stock. Value of a gain filling unmet need is three per unit. Cost of giving surplus is two. Expected gains are q(3p−2) for the payer and q(3−2p) for the supplier at quantity q and belief p. The supplier gains under the offered terms. The payer uses the material best response to that belief with a uniform tie; an independent preassigned random number selects within the tie. No decision sees future draws. Unaware uses p = 3/4. Local and pooled record counts use (3+successes)/(4+attempts) and differ in which past deliveries each buyer can observe. Autarky disables trade. The information regime and payment convention are imposed.

Each storage cap is ten units of each resource after consumption. Excess is documented loss. The exact balance identity is checked at every step for each resource. Finite hand fixtures test comparative advantage and both delivery paths before pilot evaluation.

Measure mean need satisfaction and paired pooled-minus-unaware difference, by scarcity regime, along with trade volumes and counts, production, storage losses, specialization of labor, realized link coverage, amount consumed by the worst-served agent, and Gini of per-agent consumption and final combined inventory. Two resources receive equal unit weight in both Ginis and the mean consumption welfare proxy. A zero holding total has Gini defined as zero. Shock-day satisfaction excludes worlds without shock days. Primary interpretation is exploratory and uses the world as the sampling unit. Stop after the registered trajectory counts. Record runtime and storage. Assess sign, variance, degeneracy and reference agreement before choosing a confirmatory sample size.
