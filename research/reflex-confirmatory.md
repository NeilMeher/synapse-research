# REFLEX confirmatory protocol

Freeze before execution. The 40-episode pilot took 483 ms, and the paired standard deviation of the primary contrast was 10.22 ticks. Use 400 independent six-round episodes, seed 711201. The projected half-width is about one tick and runtime under five seconds. The released record includes the first actual episode per arm, the starting and closing ledgers, and the full fee and fill tape.

All actors trade a single integer asset against an inventory-sensitive maker with a fixed two-tick half-spread and one-tick fee. Orders execute immediately against successive one-unit quotes until the limit, cash or inventory blocks the next unit. The remainder expires. There are no borrowed funds, intervening cancellations or standing customer orders. The maker's inventory moves quotes. The fundamental is a hidden contemporaneous $10,000±120$ tick value sampled afresh each round. Agents receive binary noisy clues and past/current quotes. Fundamental, momentum, contrarian and noise policies have fixed assignments and sizes. Higher and lower price impact differ in four versus one tick per maker inventory unit. Every paired arm has identical beginning capital, maker resources, order sequence and underlying fundamental/signal draws.

The primary endpoint is the paired difference, high minus low impact, in mean absolute round-end midpoint deviation from fundamental. Use a 95% normal interval on paired episode differences. Secondary outcomes are executed quantity, episode maximum midpoint drawdown, and root mean squared midpoint return. Interpret quote responsiveness and model errors in this simulator. Total marked wealth may vary with the mark. Cash and asset totals reconcile at every order. Stop at the fixed n or fail on any invariant, independent archived-ledger check, invalid configuration or the 30-second cap.
