# SCIENTIA sequential verification and selected scientific claims

For a binary observation, test a conditional success probability at most .5. Fix the alternative .75 and threshold 20 in advance. The factor \(p_1(Y_t)/p_0(Y_t)\) is 1.5 on success and .5 on failure. Its conditional mean is at most one whenever the null conditional success rate is at most .5 given all past observations and the selected test. Starting from one, the product is a nonnegative supermartingale, with equality at an independent .5 boundary. Both null outcomes have positive probability. A first crossing of level 20 is a stopping time. Ville's inequality bounds ever rejecting by .05 under this null. The guarantee requires valid **conditional** factors and a fixed claim in the fresh stage. Ordinary upper-tail exact binomial p-values are valid at a fixed horizon under independent Bernoulli observations and need multiplicity or sequential correction over repeated looks.

The fixed full comparison is a 12-observation exact enumeration with independent boundary .50, weak .60 and strong .75 success rates. It compares one final look, unadjusted repeated looks, equal per-look alpha spending and the test supermartingale. All have the same cap.
