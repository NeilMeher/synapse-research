# MIRAGE exploratory pilot

Compare five confidence policies on the same first-order answers. Train lookup calibration on 500 normal cases, then evaluate 350 new paired moments under normal and misleading cues. Training and test use different recorded random seeds. The synthetic task predicts one binary truth bit from a primary noisy report and a second hint. The selected answer always equals the primary report. Policies only change the reported probability that it is correct. The misleading regime reverses the hint's reliability. Baselines include a constant learned training rate, a style flag independent of outcomes, the declared-quality Bayesian estimator, a smoothed training-only estimate, and a data-generating oracle. Inspect the pilot variance and failure cases before writing the confirmatory protocol.
