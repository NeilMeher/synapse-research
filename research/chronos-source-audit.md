# CHRONOS source inventory, quality and reuse

| Risk | Evidence | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High, national source version | The scanned Railway Board 2024–25 Statistical Summary first page distinguishes route km from running track km. Its precise Statement 8 figures, 65,510.37 electrified and 69,439.31 total route km, round to 65,510 and 69,439 in the summary. | Use the summary series as the primary national series with exact locators. Do not combine it with differently scoped targets, gauge-specific announcements or period snapshots. |
| High, unresolved publication mismatch | For March years 2021, 2022 and 2025 the Board and 2025–26 Survey electrified stock differs beyond displayed precision. The largest latest difference is about 3,200 route km. The older Survey edition also differs for March 2021 and 2022. | Maintain the full source provenance and check both locally. No explanation by typo, programme definition or revision has been confirmed. |
| High, change in nominal geography | The RBI Handbook reports selected March stocks as published. Andhra Pradesh includes Telangana in the early period. Both rows are printed in March 2014 even though the source note describes inclusion through 2013–14. | Retain both figures, flag ambiguity and skip boundary-crossing growth. No spatial correspondence between railway zones and states is assumed. |
| High, scope and licensing | The Railway Board copyright page permits accurate attributed reproduction of its material, and its general portal terms read more broadly. The project uses limited factual cells with attribution, without reproducing the PDFs, layout, imagery or logos. The RBI permission excludes third-party copyright. The table credits the Ministry of Railways. | Selected exact factual state observations are attributed to both institutions. Neither complete RBI tables nor the source files are distributed. The Ministry of Finance's Economic Survey policy is more restrictive. Original Survey files and extracted cells stay local, with named references and conclusions in the report. |
| Medium, incomplete reporting periods | The latest Survey's 2025–26 column ends in November, and the earlier Survey's 2024–25 column ends in October. Their starred, originating and missing entries have different scope. Table 1.26 identifies changes around Konkan loading and carried versus originating measures. | No partial period enters the annual Board series, growth or ratio calculations. Different period vintages are discussed as source status only. |
| Medium, early series comparisons | RBI March 2019–2024 all-India mileage matches the Board exactly. Earlier March 2016–2018 totals differ by hundreds to over a thousand route km. | Keep the Board national series and separate RBI regional stocks. There is no backfill or forced reconciliation. |
| Medium, extraction limitations | The Board's first summary page is a raster. Manual transcription was visually checked against the original at report resolution. Eight recent cells independently match the annual detail within explicit precision. RBI CSV generation aligns each HTML row to printed year headers. The advertised state XLSX and PDF endpoints returned HTML. | Keep original retained files pinned by SHA-256, the reviewed public factual transcription and deterministic transformation. Optional local audit redownloads the source bytes and checks both publications. The early national scan does not have an independent full transcription. |
| Residual, causal validity | Available transport series and segment events have incompatible observational boundaries. | No historical effect estimation. See the separate [causal readiness register](chronos-causal-readiness.md). |

The first attempt to read the RBI HTML used an invalid Python encoding alias and did not enter the data. The first boundary fixture expected an unavailable March 2014 Telangana observation. The original publisher table shows an actual number, so the fixture and metadata now preserve the observation and flag the time-specific scope. Neither issue altered a previously archived result.

The sources are the Railway Board and Reserve Bank of India publications stated in the manifest. The statistical appendix cross-check links are retained with exact table and page locations. Original PDFs and RBI source HTML stay at their publishers. Independent human source and copyright review is pending.
