# MORPHEUS confirmatory protocol

Study `morpheus-adaptive-reliability-confirmatory-002` · synthetic evidence B

## Question and endpoint

How much decision loss comes from the memory weighting rule and how much comes from the audit observation model? The sole primary endpoint is the mean within-history difference in **64-step incorrect-decision share** between event-time EWMA (.35) and cumulative pseudo-count averaging, both corrected using the known audit accuracy, in the .80-accuracy abrupt failure and restoration environment. A negative difference favors EWMA. Both start from .86 reliability and invert the same audit channel. The contrast changes temporal weights and how the starting estimate is retained. The comparator accumulates across the whole observable history, so it is a nonforgetting learner. Use the separately registered fixed .86 baseline to interpret whether either update is beneficial relative to unchanged trust.

A secondary difference-in-differences compares the change from treating the audit bit as truth to known-channel correction in EWMA versus cumulative averaging. The policy formula, threshold and timing remain the same inside each correction comparison. Additional secondary comparisons quantify restoration, stationarity, drift, near-chance audits, suspect audit calibration and arrival-time approximation. The complete choices are machine-readable. Mechanism-by-environment summaries without a registered contrast are descriptive. [Frozen parameters](morpheus-confirmatory/protocol.json)

## Data-generating process and visibility

Generate 384 independent seeded histories per environment with common underlying uniform draws across the six environments and all policies. Each step consumes six uniforms in order: truth, suspect correctness, alternative correctness, audit selection, audit correctness and long-delay selection. Truth is a fair binary draw. The alternative is .69 reliable. The suspect is .86 until time 20, drops to .22 through time 41 under burst and restores to .86 at time 42. The drift condition descends linearly across that window. The stationary source stays at .86. Reports for the two sources are independent conditional on truth and rates. About 60% of suspect reports are audited. The auditor independently flips true correctness with probability \(1-\eta\). Accuracy is .80, .60 or .50 according to the environment. Delay is two or six steps, with the six-step frequency increased in a prespecified stress condition. The schedule is independent of truth and correctness. Audits can arrive out of order, together or after the horizon.

Each policy sees the same current reports and audits received through decision time. It has no truth or simulated regime state. When reports disagree, it takes the suspect report only when its estimate exceeds .69 by more than \(10^{-12}\). Otherwise it takes the alternative. On agreement it takes the common report. Loss is one for an incorrect decision and zero for a correct decision. It is assessed by an evaluator outside the policy. Fixed control reliability is part of the design. Audits have identical exogenous budget across policies, with no extra query or financial cost.

The workload yields about 2.8 million bounded policy decisions. Across 16 development paths, the archived uncorrected EWMA-versus-cumulative paired loss SD was 5.87 percentage points, suggesting a planning half-width of 0.59 points with 384 histories. The new contrast can have different variance because correction changes the estimators. The target size of 384 is fixed independent of achieved significance. The seed differs from the development seed and inputs exist only after preregistration.

## Abstractions and uncertainty

Let \(A\) be the recorded bit and \(q\) the underlying success probability. For known accuracy \(\eta>.5\), update with \(A^*=(A-(1-\eta))/(2\eta-1)\). Intermediate estimates may exceed the probability range. Clip the final estimate to [0,1] for routing and leave intermediate accumulators unaltered. A misspecified arm assumes .90. The naive arm assumes perfect audits and reads the recorded bit directly. At \(\eta=.5\) the corrected running estimators skip updating. The correct Markov channel has equal emissions across regimes, so transition prediction proceeds without observation information. HMM likelihoods use the existing hidden-state filter. Its regime law is a modeling assumption because the actual simulator uses fixed transitions or drift. Markov versus EWMA changes the temporal model and is interpreted as a bundled comparison.

Cumulative and rolling computations use the mean of the transformed observations blended with four prior-weight units at .86. A rolling estimate uses eight eligible audits. These are weighted estimators. Beta–Bernoulli conjugacy generally fails under known audit error. EWMA is initialized at .86 and weights each accepted audit by recency in the selected ordering. Event ordering is by original report time over currently visible audits. Arrival ordering is by arrival time with original report time as the tie-break. They weight by eligible audit count. The Markov policy replays original event-time emissions and transitions. Exact small path fixtures check no-audit, chance-audit, simultaneous, out-of-order and late arrivals.

The experimental unit is an independent history. Pair each policy outcome and each interaction within history. The interval is a two-sided 95% paired normal interval using sample variance across history-level differences. All 384 outcomes contribute, including equal-policy histories. Report the exact summed error-count difference with the mean and interval. Secondary intervals are marginal, unadjusted diagnostics. Policy contrasts do not pool environments because those conditions share draws. Full, failure and restoration losses remain separate. Abort publication if replays, asset hashes, exact fixtures, original and independent paired analysis, or information-visibility checks fail. Both separate implementations must agree on all realized decisions. Tolerance for real-valued verification is absolute \(10^{-12}\).

## Prior work

The binary error channel uses standard outcome-misclassification algebra. Exponential weighting and finite HMM filtering are established methods. This study's contribution is the controlled comparison of audit correction, memory weighting and timing under shared histories, accompanied by explicit recovery and stationary controls.
