# EPISTEME pilot protocol

Use 100 matched tasks per regime and seed 390921381 to check controls, execution cost and paired dispersion. The independent unit is a hidden-law task. Known laws are sampled uniformly among the four NOESIS families. Outside-library laws use a constant, sin(πx) and x² together. All coefficients are independent unit normals. Each observation has independent N(0, 0.2²) noise, including the first at x=0.

Random selection without replacement and greatest predictive-mean disagreement receive six matched discovery measurements each. The policy selects its next site from past discovery data and private random choices only. The observation likelihood multiplies conditional Gaussian terms. Measurement-policy factors given past data have no unknown coefficients and cancel from posterior odds. Four disjoint fixed validation and four further disjoint fixed replication measurements are collected under every policy. Freeze the posterior and leading predictor after discovery. Allow a family claim at posterior ≥0.70 within the candidate library, a validation claim when the first residual block passes, and a final accepted claim when both blocks pass. A block passes if its four-point chi-square residual tail is at least 0.05 under the sharp hypothesis of correct fixed means with known noise. The test does not condition on or reuse either block to tune the predictor. Failure to reject yields an operational pass. A correct claim identifies the sampled family, and every family claim is incorrect in an outside-library world.

Calculate diagnostic RMSE against noiseless truth on a third disjoint grid, independent first-confidence timing with censoring at seven, acceptance and non-acceptance rates and paired differences. Compare disagreement minus random on correct accepted in library and false proposed outside library. At pilot stage use the intervals descriptively. Stop after the specified tasks and fail for broken disjointness, reference disagreement beyond numerical precision or runtime over 30 seconds.
