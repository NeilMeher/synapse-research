# ENERGIA registered deterministic infrastructure scenario

Represent one modular, hypothetical 128-GPU L40S service for eight operating years. All cash values are stipulated scenario USD on a 2024 denomination convention. Prices stay nominal and flat except the explicitly decreasing spot rate. The manufacturer maximum of 350 W per GPU is a constraint. GPU idle and active workload watts, hardware costs, staffing, water, commercial contract terms and financing are assumptions. No project location, power connection, legally applicable tariff or market quote is asserted.

Use 8,760 hours, a persistent idle baseline, the workload's marginal accelerator power, and separate spot and reserved billable capacity. The reserved block is a take-or-pay assumption that releases to the spot pool after the contract. Apply one PUE to IT energy and peak power as a model convention. Water is site usage under liters per kWh IT. The site total does not describe a withdrawal permit. The simplified monthly demand charge uses the larger of contract kVA and design kVA and assumes this level in all operating months. This is a billing approximation.

Use annual pre-tax cash flow. The capex ledger has six disjoint categories. Initial working capital is released at the end and facility disposal values net of decommission costs are separate. Replacement costs reduce CFADS in the specified operating year. Senior debt is drawn at commissioning, fully amortizing over the stipulated tenor. Construction-period funds come from equity until draw. Reserve funds can pay debt service, then residual reserve releases at the end. The scheduled equity cash flow and its implied additional funding are recorded even after a payment gap. A first gap is a scenario signal. No interim bailout, legal default waterfall, taxes, tax shield or insolvency model is assumed.

Compare baseline, all equity, combined stress, replacement at end, delay alone, and shorter reserved contract. Compute two utilization grids (spot bill fraction × energy tariff or spot price), financing rate × hardware price, bounded single-parameter project NPV break evens, and a schedule NPV profile. Hold engineering workload fixed when varying bill fraction. Report ambiguous IRR where cash flows change sign more than once and provide a profile rather than asserting uniqueness. Register a finite candidate set with explicit capital, electrical, rack, water, coverage and payment-gap constraints. Minimize cost per billed GPU-hour, site water and peak kVA jointly. Check the intentionally infeasible control. No assumed probability distribution or investment recommendation.
